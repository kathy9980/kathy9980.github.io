Kexin Song

Postdoctoral Associate · Yale School of the Environment

I study how ecosystems and societies respond to disturbance and global environmental change. My research combines Earth observation, field measurements, causal inference, and geospatial AI to make environmental change more observable, explainable, and actionable.

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the SPIRES Lab at Yale, working with Dr. Luke C. Sanford. I earned my Ph.D. at the University of Connecticut under the guidance of Dr. Zhe Zhu in the GERS Lab.
