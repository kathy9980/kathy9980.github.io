Teaching & mentorship

Making complex science meaningful

I equip students with the conceptual foundations, technical skills, and critical judgment needed to address environmental challenges with confidence.

Teaching philosophy

I connect course content to real-world applications, use project-based labs and discussion to make learning active, and develop visual and interactive materials that support students from diverse backgrounds.

My goal is not only to teach tools, but to help students become independent thinkers who can select methods thoughtfully, interpret evidence responsibly, and clearly communicate the findings and limitations of their analyses. I approach artificial intelligence as a learning aid, never a substitute for reasoning.
